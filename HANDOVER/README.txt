MD CONVENT PUBLIC SCHOOL — SCHOOL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (SMS)
Official Standalone Windows Desktop Application | Version 2.0.0
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This handover package contains the official, verified Windows installer for
MD Convent SMS.

The application is completely offline, self-contained, and engineered for daily
school operations, including:
  * Student Admissions & Bio-data Management (NC to Class 12)
  * School Fee Collection with Multi-Month Selection & Instant Receipts
  * Full Transport Module: Bus Stops, Student Routing & Transport Receipts
  * Single-Row Consolidated Fee Reminders & Defaulter Tracking
  * Multi-Session Management (2026-27, 2027-28) with Safe Student Promotion
  * Periodic & Term Marks Entry (UT1, Half Yearly, UT2, Annual) + Report Cards
  * Attendance Registers & Monthly Summaries
  * Financial & Collection Reports with Excel-compatible CSV Exports
  * Built-in Automatic Atomic Database Persistence & Safety Snapshots


2. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Operating System:  Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  * Processor:         Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 or higher
  * Memory (RAM):      2 GB minimum (4 GB recommended)
  * Storage Space:     250 MB free disk space
  * Display:           1024 x 680 minimum resolution (1366 x 768 or higher recommended)
  * Internet:          NOT REQUIRED (100% offline; zero cloud or network dependencies)
  * Prerequisites:     NONE. Electron runtime, Chromium and Node libraries are fully
                       bundled inside the installer executable.


3. QUICK START (3 SIMPLE STEPS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1: Open the "Installer" folder.
  STEP 2: Double-click "MD_Convent_SMS_Setup_v2.0.exe".
  STEP 3: Follow the setup wizard (keep "Create Desktop Shortcut" checked)
          and click Finish.

The app will launch immediately and place a shortcut named "MD Convent SMS"
on your Windows Desktop and Start Menu.
